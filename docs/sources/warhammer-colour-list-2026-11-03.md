# Warhammer Colour Paint Range: Full List with Approximate Hex Codes (as of 3 November 2026)

## Read this first

- This file lists the paints that Games Workshop (GW) sells under the name Warhammer Colour and Warhammer Tone Pro.
- The reader of this file cannot visit web sites. This file holds all the data needed.
- **The facts about brand name, range types, and Tone Pro come from GW primary sources.**
- **The paint-by-paint names for the older ranges come from one secondary source** (Brush & Wargames, a paint database). GW's own shop pages block automatic access. No primary paint-by-paint list could be read.
- The secondary list was checked against GW sources where possible. See "Checks and known gaps".
- This edition adds approximate hex codes. See the hex section below.
- Scope: paints and mediums only. Paint sets, brushes, and tools are not listed, except the Tone Pro mediums and drybrushes.

## Hex codes: read this before you use them

- **No official GW hex list was found.** The values below are **unofficial approximations**. The Paint Warhammer app may hold official colour data. This was not checked.
- Source: a community list on the Bolter & Chainsword forum (posted 1 January 2019 by an administrator). The post says the codes are approximations from colour swatches found online. It does not say how the swatches were made.
- The list covers the Citadel range **as it was in 2019**. It does not cover the 2022 Contrast paints, the 2022 Shades, or Tone Pro. These paints show a dash (—).
- One hex code belongs to one colour name. It is the same in every format (Base, Layer, Air, Spray, Dry). The list author gave Layer priority, then Spray, Air, and Dry.
- Shades, Technical, Texture, Glaze, and metallic paints look different from their hex codes. A hex code cannot show gloss, transparency, or metallic shine. Use these paints' hex codes as a rough guide only.
- Some values look wrong for the paint. Example: Blackfire Earth is shown as orange (#F8902F). Check any value that matters before you use it.
- Contrast is almost empty (2 of 61). The 2019 list was made before most Contrast paints existed.
- Do not call these values "official" or "measured".

## 1. Brand name

- On 6 March 2026, GW renamed **Citadel Colour** to **Warhammer Colour**. GW says the paint is the same paint. Only the name on the pot changed.
- GW is replacing the old pots with the new branding over "weeks and months". Shops can hold both names at the same time.
- Many older paint names in shops and guides still start with "Citadel". Treat "Citadel X" and "Warhammer Colour X" as the same paint.
- GW Colour sites: paint.warhammer.com (guides and app) and warhammer.com/paint (shop).

## 3. Facts from GW primary sources

- GW paint types named on paint.warhammer.com: Base, Layer, Shade, Dry, Contrast, Technical, Spray, and Warhammer Tone Pro.
- GW describes Base, Layer, Shade, Technical, and Contrast as the core Warhammer Colour types.
- **Warhammer Tone Pro** is a new range. It has 30 colours. Each colour has 5 numbered tones. Tone 1 is the darkest. Tone 5 is the lightest. In most colours, tone 3 is the midtone. This gives 150 paints.
  - GW showed the range on 18 September 2026 (Big Autumn Preview).
  - Pre-orders opened on 10 October 2026.
  - In-store release was Saturday 24 October 2026.
- **Tone Pro mediums** (two):
  - Blending Medium: thins the paint and gives more drying time for blends.
  - Shading Medium: turns any Tone Pro paint into a shade or glaze.
- **Warhammer Pro Tone Drybrushes** (four tools, not paints): Large, Medium, Small, Extra Small.
- Paint Warhammer is the official GW companion app for Warhammer Colour and Tone Pro. It has a comparison tool.
- 2022 range change (GW, 13 June 2022):
  - 25 new Contrast paints were added.
  - All Shades were reformulated. Seven new Shades were added. These are Mortarion Grime, Tyran Blue, Berserker Bloodshade, Soulblight Grey, and three others that GW did not name in the text.
  - White Scar spray was added.
  - The four Gloss Shades **left the range**: Nuln Oil Gloss, Agrax Earthshade Gloss, Reikland Fleshshade Gloss, and Cryptek Armourshade Gloss.

## 6. Checks and known gaps

1. **No GW paint-by-paint page was read.** warhammer.com blocks automatic access. The paint-by-paint names are from one secondary database. They may have errors or old entries.
2. **Totals agree across sources.** The database total (397 + 150) matches the sum of its range counts. The Layer count (93) matches a second retailer guide. GW says "more than 300 colours" in older text. This is consistent.
3. **Probable old entries.** The Shade list holds four Gloss Shades. GW said these four left the range in 2022. The true current Shade count may be 19, not 23. This is not confirmed. Other ranges may also hold old entries.
4. **Not paints.** Some list entries are mediums, thinners, or varnishes (Air Cleaner, Air Caste Thinner, Purity Seal, Munitorum Varnish, Lahmian Medium, Contrast Medium, Ardcoat, Imperial Primer, Liquid Green Stuff). Count them as "range products", not colours.
5. **Pot sizes and prices** are not confirmed from GW. Do not quote them as official.
6. **Date conflict resolved.** The 18 September article says shoppers can "try" Tone Pro in stores from 26 September. Pre-orders opened 10 October. GW's 20 October article says in-store sale starts "Saturday", which is 24 October 2026.
7. **Possible new products after 24 October 2026** are not covered.
8. **To close gap 1:** compare this list with the Paint Warhammer app or warhammer.com/paint by hand.
