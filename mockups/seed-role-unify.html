Seed role × live palette — three ways to unify

Today "Main | Accent" is a toolbar segmented control, far from the palette bar where its effect lands. All three directions below delete that control and express the seed's role on the scheme itself. Seed: Faerzress Purple #87146E, complementary, accent mode.

1 · Docked seed chip — your paint physically sits in its role's column

The pick is a chip (swatch + name) docked under the column it seeds. The other eligible column shows a dashed “seed here” dock — tap it (or drag the chip) and the whole scheme rebuilds around the new role. State and control are the same object: where the chip sits IS the mode.

Strongest unification. Reads as “my paint plays this role”, the toolbar loses a widget (mobile win), and dragging the chip is a natural later upgrade. Cost: the docks add one row of height to two columns.

2 · The hero badge becomes the flip

The hero already stamps your pick with a “main / accent” pill. Make that pill the control: tapping it flips the role (it shows the flip affordance on hover). The toolbar control disappears; the palette bar is untouched.

3 · Anchor markers in the column corners

Each column that can hold the seed (Primary, Accent) wears a small ⚓ radio in its corner. The filled one marks where your pick anchors; tapping the hollow one moves it. The seed chip stays on the hero — the palette only carries the two-dot radio.
