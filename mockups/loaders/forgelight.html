Ember bokeh

Leaning into what it wanted to be: a defocused forge. Three depth-of-field layers of bokeh discs — bright-rimmed the way real lens bokeh is — drift upward, and crackle sparks fire several times more often, snapping a moment of focus through the blur.

3 DOF layers: far pin-points → near wide discs with a bokeh rim (bright ring, soft core) · rise + sine sway + two-sine flicker · crackle streaks every ~1.1s, up to two live

Shipping notes — dynamic to the environment, light on resources

  • Container-driven. Every effect is render(ctx, w, h, state) — no fixed geometry. In the app a host element's box (via ResizeObserver) sets the frame, exactly as the frame switchers do here; particle and cell counts scale with area, DPR is capped at 2.
  • They only run during their moment. Loaders animate while a real load is in flight and stop at 1 (the annealed frame is static). Sparks are a ~1s one-shot. The bokeh loop pauses on document.hidden, when scrolled out of view, and under reduced motion.
  • Opt-in, off by default. A "Forge effects" control in the ⋯ settings popover (a segmented off · on per §3.5 — the theme switch stays the only toggle-switch), persisted through store.js prefs, offered in the dark theme. Off = the current instant states; nothing regresses.
  • No dependency, no drift. Seeded PRNG + one heat ramp derived from --accent; swap the ramp for the Playful palette and the same choreography serves the light theme later.
