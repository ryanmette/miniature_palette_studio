The handle: no “⌕ Search” chip

The chip said “Search” even when nothing was searched, and the search field is one pull away. Proposed: the handle is only the pill. A chip shows only when something hides paints, so a filtered shelf is never a mystery (the reason the chip was there, §3.6).

Recommendation: C. It keeps the panel as short as today, Select keeps its word beside Filters (the two actions on the shelf together), and Grid | List become two plain icons beside the status filter. A is the clearest if one more row is fine. B is the most compact, but three unlabelled icons and a renamed “Shopping list” cost more than they save. For the handle: the pill alone, and the chip only when a search or a filter is on.
