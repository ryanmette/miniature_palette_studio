Paints picker — four directions and a recommendation

The problem (Ryan, 2026-10-27): the Studio’s ☰ Paints drawer is “redundant and confusing for users”. On a 390px phone, the seed toolbar is 3 rows (178px). The open drawer adds 4 rows of controls (194px) and shows only about 3½ paints. Most of its controls exist somewhere else too.

An audit read the code and the docs. Four agents each designed one direction, and three judges scored them: the painter, the constitution and accessibility, and the build. All three judges chose the same base. The recommended mix is at the end, and you can try it: it runs on 134 real paints from all ten brands, with the app’s own ΔE 2000 code.

Today

One job (choose the colour that starts the scheme) has two text inputs. One drawer copies the Shelf’s filters and its import and export. The first phone screen is all controls.

Bugs found on the way (reproduced)

  • Small phones open scrolled down. At 360px and 375px wide the page loads about 372–386px down, past the toolbar. The tab strip’s scrollIntoView (app.js:1242) also scrolls the page. At 390px it is fine.
  • A tap on ☰ Paints raises the keyboard. It always focuses the search, so the keyboard covers the paints.
  • A pasted #9A1115 does nothing. The hex box keeps 6 characters, so it holds 9A111.
  • A paint name in the hex box makes a colour. “Abaddon Black” seeds #ABADDB.
  • Marks do not always refresh. After a mark, the hero, the drawer or the Studio can show the old state, until the next full render. With Use my collection on, even the matches can be stale.
  • The drawer is heavy. It holds 3,225 buttons (3,232 tab stops) and rebuilds them on every colour change, also while it is closed.

Choices for you (the judges disagreed)

  • Live hex. Today a hex seeds as you type. The proposal keeps that for # + 6 digits, and asks for Enter for anything else (a bare 9A1115, or #9A1). One judge wanted Enter for everything, so a half-typed hex never flashes a wrong scheme. The proposal avoids that flash, because a 3-digit hex never seeds live.
  • Marking in the Studio. The proposal uses a long-press menu (no new buttons). Two judges preferred a small “✓ own · + buy” pair on each Plan row. That pair is easier to find, but it adds more small buttons, and today’s buy buttons are already under 44px.
  • Recent picks. The proposal keeps them for this visit only, so they are not new saved data and need no backup or delete. One judge wanted them saved.
  • Browse grid or rows. The proposal uses a grid to browse (you look by colour) and rows to search (you read names). The other option is rows everywhere.

Fixed by this change

  • The keyboard rises only when you tap the field, never on ▾.
  • A pasted #9A1115 works, and “Abaddon Black” can never become #ABADDB.
  • The list exists only while it is open: one tab stop instead of 3,232, and no rebuild of 3,225 buttons on every colour change.
  • One render('marks') path: a mark anywhere updates the hero, the palette, the open list and the Shelf.
  • The small-phone load scroll (app.js:1242), which can also be fixed now, on its own.
