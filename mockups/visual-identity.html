Palette Studio — visual identity and UX pass

This page is a design document. The app does not load it. It holds the audit, three design directions, the UX pass, the judges' scores and a recommendation.

Status: waiting for Ryan. Nothing in src/ changes until Ryan picks a direction (Part 5 of the brief). The branch starts from the head of PR #39 (claude/nice-ptolemy-wstdsl), because PR #39 is still open.

1. Audit — what makes the app look "made by AI"

Method: a Playwright script (mockups/visual-identity/capture.cjs) opened the app on PR #39's head with one fixed seed (?p=citadel-base-mephiston-red). It took 24 surfaces at 360×640, 390×844, 768×1024 (touch), 1025×800 (touch), 1180×820 and 1280×800, in light and dark: 288 screenshots. The same script measured the computed styles of every visible control. The page shows two screenshots per surface (390 light and 1280 dark). The script makes the full set again in about ten minutes.

Ryan's own two causes are real and measurable: one soft radius, and one button size. They are only the start. The bigger tell is that every default of a 2024 component kit is still in place at the same time: violet accent, Inter and Space Grotesk, a card for every region, soft two-layer shadows, pill badges, micro-caps labels and Unicode icons. Any one of them is fine. All of them together is the "AI" look.

1.2 Two problems the audit found that are not style

  • Dark accent = dark warning. The brass --accent:#C2912F is ΔE 2.7 from --warning:#C9923A. They are the same colour to the eye, so a "Fair" or "Loose" match tag looks like a selected control. This breaks §3.5 ("single-meaning"). All three directions fix it the same way: --warning becomes #E0803A (ΔE 14.8 from the brass, 16.4 from --danger, 6.15:1 on --surface).
  • Field edges are 1.4:1. The seed field and the Shelf search are drawn only by --border, 1.22:1 (light) and 1.37:1 (dark) on the surface. WCAG 2.1 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for the visual edge that identifies a field. All three directions add an --edge token (3.4–3.7:1) for fields and selects only.
