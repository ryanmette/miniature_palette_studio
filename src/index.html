About Palette Studio

A single-page tool for miniature painters: pick or explore a colour and get colour harmonies matched to real paints you can buy — with cross-brand equivalents, colour-blindness simulation and WCAG contrast checks.

How the matching works — and why it’s honest

Colours are converted sRGB → CIELAB (D65) and matched by CIEDE2000 (ΔE 2000); harmonies rotate hue in HSL. That maths is exact and unit-tested. But paint hex values are approximate (most are community-sourced and tagged “approx”), so every match shows its ΔE and a plain-language quality label — “target vs pot” always surfaces the gap and never implies a perfect match.

The Help page explains how the matching works in plain words, and lists the keyboard shortcuts.

Data & credits

The dataset (3,225 paints across ten brands — Citadel, Vallejo, The Army Painter, AK Interactive, AMMO by Mig, Two Thin Coats, Reaper, Scale75, P3 and Pro Acryl) is imported from the community dataset Arcturus5404/miniature-paints, used under the MIT License, © 2022 Rick Fleuren. Privacy & support

Runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no server, no tracking, and no external requests. Your shelf (owned paints, your shopping list) and your settings stay on this device. Read the full privacy policy.
